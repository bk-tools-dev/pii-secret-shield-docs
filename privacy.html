Privacy Policy – PII & Secret Shield for Jira

Vendor: BK-Tools
Last updated: November 2026

Zero External Data Egress ("Runs on Atlassian"): PII & Secret Shield runs 100% natively on Atlassian Forge infrastructure. Customer ticket data never leaves your Atlassian Cloud instance and is never transmitted to BK-Tools servers or any third-party AI/LLM provider.

1. Data Accessed

When installed in Jira Cloud, the app accesses issue summaries, descriptions, and comments strictly via Atlassian's authorized Jira REST API (read:jira-work, write:jira-work) solely to detect and redact sensitive PII and API secrets.

2. Data Storage (Zero Personal Data Stored)

Configuration settings (auto-redact toggle, ignored internal email domains, custom prefixes, retention threshold) and a strictly anonymous audit log (timestamp, issue key, action type such as manual-panel or retention-scheduler, category counts — never user account IDs, raw secrets, or unmasked PII) are stored exclusively within your installation's isolated Atlassian Forge Key-Value Store (storage:app).

3. Third-Party Subprocessors

None. The app declares zero external network egress permissions in its Forge manifest. All compute and storage reside within Atlassian Cloud under your existing Atlassian Data Residency and Data Processing Addendum.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Uninstalling the app from your Jira Cloud site automatically removes app access, and Forge-hosted app storage follows Atlassian's standard app data lifecycle. Audit log entries stored in Forge KVS are capped at the 100 most recent masked events.
